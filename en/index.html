Know what is in every box

Boxes lists what is inside your boxes, bags and crates, so you never have to open them to look. Add things by typing, by voice or from a photo, print a label with a QR code and scan it later.

Everything for packing, moving and finding things

Pack

  • Boxes and rooms

    Rooms, categories and colours of your own, numbered boxes.

  • Add by voice

    “Two sweaters, a charger, cookbooks”: you check the list first.

  • Add from a photo

    From the camera or the photo library.

  • Moves as projects

    One box per room, packing progress, receiving and unpacking.

Find

  • QR labels

    Print with AirPrint or save a PDF. Readable without the app.

  • Scan a code

    See the contents of a box straight away.

  • Search

    “Where is the drill?” Filter by room, category or box.

  • iCloud sync

    Optional, free, in your own private iCloud.
